# ECP-OS Home

> You study by talking to your AI agent. Open this folder in your agent and say **"Let's study"**.
> This page shows where you are. You can tick boxes here; you never need to edit anything else.

## Talk to your agent

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Let's study" | Today's session: review, practice, and a quick log |
| "Quiz me" | Review the cards that are due |
| "Role-play ordering food" | Practice a scene with the agent |
| "Save this video: *link*" | Add a learning source to your Inbox |
| "Check my pronunciation" | Pronunciation check with speech-to-text |
| "Weekly review" | Look back at the week and plan the next one |
