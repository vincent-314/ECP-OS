# ECP-OS

**A self-study system for Mandarin Chinese, run by you and your AI agent.**

You decide where you want to be in six months. Your agent turns that into monthly goals,
weekly tasks and daily practice, gives you feedback, and keeps every record up to date.
Every week and every month you look back together and adjust. You just study and talk.

> Version 0.2.0: all workflows are written and being tested.

## What you need

- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) (free) to see your dashboard, vocabulary and progress.
- An AI agent that can read and edit files in a folder on your computer.
  ECP-OS does not depend on one product; any capable agent can run it.

## Start in three steps

1. Open this folder as a vault in Obsidian, then open **Home.md**.
2. Open the same folder in your AI agent.
3. Say **"Hi, let's start."** Your agent will ask you a few rounds of questions (about 20–30 minutes)
   and set up your six-month plan, your first month's goals and your first week's tasks.

## Your study loop

| When | Say to your agent | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Every study day | "Let's study" | Review due cards, practice a scene, log the session |
| Any time | "Save this video: *link*" | The source goes into your Inbox with a short summary |
| After speaking or writing | "Check my pronunciation" / "Here's my essay" | Feedback, corrections, and mistakes logged |
| Once a week | "Weekly review" | What improved, what fell behind, next week's tasks |
| End of each month | "Monthly review" | Goals scored, next month's goals set |
| After months 3 and 6 | "Quarterly review" | Check the big picture against your vision |

## What's in the folders

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `Home.md` | Your dashboard |
| `01-profile` | Your learner profile and six-month vision |
| `02-okr` | Monthly objectives and key results |
| `03-tasks` | Your tasks, each linked to one objective |
| `04-daily` | Daily study logs |
| `05-reviews` | Weekly, monthly and quarterly reviews |
| `06-library` | Your words, sentence patterns and scenes |
| `07-inbox` | Videos, articles and podcasts you saved |
| `08-output` | Your speaking and writing practice, with feedback |
| `09-attachments` | Recordings, images and PDFs |
| `00-system` | How the system works. Your agent reads it; you don't need to |

## Good to know

- **You never need to edit properties.** Your agent keeps records up to date. You can still tick boxes,
  rename tasks or delete tasks in Obsidian; your changes always win.
- **Pronunciation feedback** works without a teacher: you read a sentence into your phone's or computer's
  Chinese voice input, and your agent compares what it typed with what you meant to say.
  If you have a teacher, their notes can be added too.
- **The sample data** (one scene about ordering food, ten words, five patterns) shows how things look.
  Your agent offers to remove it when you start.
- **Upgrading:** replace the `00-system` folder with the new version. Your own data lives in the
  other folders and is not touched.
